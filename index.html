About

I'm a builder at heart — I like turning ideas into real things, whether that's a business, a song, or a website. I'm drawn to experimentation and figuring out how things work, and I'm usually happiest in the middle of a project. This site is where I share what I'm building and learning.

Livermore, CA · Childhood

Biking, Legos, and Sunday church

I grew up in Livermore with my brother, in a household that valued activity, curiosity, and family traditions like church every Sunday. I was always building or exploring something — K'NEX, Legos, biking around the city, hours lost in RuneScape, and plenty of baseball, hockey, paintball, and airsoft with friends.

Livermore, CA · High School

Training, music, and my first business books

My interests shifted toward self-improvement and creativity. I got dedicated to training in the gym and, around the same time, discovered music production — I became fascinated with hip hop and electronic music. I also started reading Think and Grow Rich and How to Win Friends and Influence People, which sparked a curiosity about entrepreneurship and sales.

Los Angeles, CA · Icon Collective

Moving for music production school

After high school I moved to LA to attend Icon Collective, focusing on developing my technical and creative skills as a producer.

Livermore, CA · Bankhead Theater

Live audio and stage work

Back in Livermore, I worked at the Bankhead Theater doing live audio and stage work — experience behind the scenes in live production.

Manteca, CA · COVID Era

Sales at T-Mobile, first steps into ecommerce

I moved to Manteca and worked in sales at T-Mobile, which sharpened my communication and customer-relationship skills. At the same time I kept up my self-education and started building my first ecommerce businesses.

San Diego, CA · Today

Prime Wine Coolers, and staying curious

Today my main focus is building and growing Prime Wine Coolers. San Diego has also been a great place to stay active — I ride bikes around the city and keep learning from friends, recently diving into cooking and real estate.
